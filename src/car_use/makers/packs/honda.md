# Honda / Acura

## Talking to the car
- Generic OBD-II on US models from 1996; CAN on all US models from 2008. [common]
- Dealer tool: HDS (Honda Diagnostic System, now i-HDS). [common]
- Honda shows service due through its own Maintenance Minder; generic OBD does not report it. [common]

## Known patterns
- K24 engines (around 2008-2012 Accord and CR-V) are known for a rattle for a second or two on cold start from the VTC actuator. It usually sets no code; a person has to hear it. [common]
