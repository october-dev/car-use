# Hyundai / Kia

## Talking to the car
- Generic OBD-II on US models from 1996; CAN on all US models from 2008. [common]
- Dealer tool: GDS (Global Diagnostic System). [common]
- India: Hyundai Motor India (VIN prefix MAL) and Kia India (MZB). BS-VI models (from April 2020) support OBD-II. [common]

## Known patterns
- Theta II engines (2.0 L and 2.4 L, roughly 2011-2019 Sonata, Optima, Santa Fe, Sportage and others) were the subject of large US recalls for connecting-rod bearing failure. Hyundai and Kia added a knock-sensor detection software update. A knocking noise or oil-pressure warning on these: stop driving and check recall status with the dealer by VIN. [common]
